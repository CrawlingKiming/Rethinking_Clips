# Finite-time ESS convergence self-review

## Revised section outline

1. The ESS reliability bridge expresses permissive-gradient MSE through effective support and weighted gradient scale.
2. Smoothness converts realized gradient error into a pointwise one-step improvement guarantee.
3. Telescoping that guarantee yields finite-time first-order stationarity controlled by average gradient error.
4. Prior work confirms the same estimator-error principle for off-policy policy gradients, conventional stochastic policy gradients, and biased stochastic gradients.
5. Under conditional independent sampling, the ESS reliability bound enters the finite-time stationarity guarantee directly.
6. Reused-rollout optimization retains the pathwise gradient-error guarantee, while an ESS substitution requires adaptive-data control and is therefore tested empirically.

The revision establishes the optimization role that the paper previously asserted only locally.
