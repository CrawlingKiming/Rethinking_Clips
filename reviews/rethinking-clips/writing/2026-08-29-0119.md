# Writing review: theory and one-step narrative

**Status:** PASS IN SCOPE

## Diagnosis of the previous flow

The previous draft had the correct ingredients but presented them in the order
they were derived rather than the order a reader needs them. The Raw MSE
calculation appeared without a motivating question; general notation became
token-specific halfway through the theory; two improvement results appeared
without stating their different jobs; implementation details interrupted the
estimator comparison; and the Gaussian example began with calculations rather
than its purpose. This produced local correctness without a visible argument.

## Writing standard

Good writing for this paper has five properties:

1. Each section answers one reader question.
2. Definitions appear before the theorem sequence that uses them.
3. A theorem is introduced by its purpose and followed by its operational
   meaning.
4. Each paragraph advances one claim through evidence to implication.
5. Main-text algebra serves the argument; derivations and estimator-specific
   implementation details belong in the appendix.

Scope conditions are stated once at the relevant boundary. They are not
repeated as defensive caveats.

## Reverse outline after revision

1. **Introduction:** delayed failure raises a finite-sample support question
   that policy-deviation bounds do not answer.
2. **Preliminaries:** factor ratios, scores, advantages, Raw, PPO, conditioning,
   and normalized ESS are defined before the theory.
3. **Raw reliability:** the exact Raw MSE identifies the effective count
   \(N\rho_k\); a high-probability radius and smoothness produce a realized
   one-step guarantee.
4. **Estimator selection:** bias and variance determine the MSE crossover.
5. **Update selection:** alignment and update scale determine the full
   fixed-step certificate; this theorem is the oracle-selection result.
6. **Exact Gaussian bandit:** closed-form moments separate the MSE crossover,
   update crossover, and abstention boundary for \(N=32\).
7. **Language-model evidence:** the gate preserves the high-support permissive
   regime and activates protection as support contracts.
8. **Appendices:** Gaussian integration, coefficient-rule details, Optdigits,
   diagnostics, and proofs support the main line without interrupting it.

The reader-facing chain is:

effective support -> Raw reliability -> estimator risk -> update certificate -> update choice.
