## Reviewer-facing conclusion

The revised paper no longer sets the calibrated boundary and the fixed 0.1 heuristic against each other as if they served the same objective. The oracle study verifies the theorem and identifies the configuration-specific MSE crossover. Proposition 10 then states the conditional guarantee available to any observable gate. The on-trajectory diagnostic verifies the low-ESS premise for 0.1, and repeated optimization shows that this sparse trigger is an effective bridge to RLVR while preserving the stronger tail-risk interpretation of the calibrated boundary.
