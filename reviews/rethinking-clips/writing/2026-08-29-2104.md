# Writing review: Section 4 reliability and certificate flow

**Status:** PASS IN SCOPE

## Argument flow

- Section 4.1 preserves the author's intended order: a generic gradient-error
  lemma first, normalized ESS as the IS-specific error control second, and the
  combined one-step improvement theorem last.
- Section 4.2 retains only the reused quantities $m_k(u)$ and
  $B_k(u;\eta_k)$. The redundant global $\mu_k(u)$, $v_k(u)$, and $s_k(u)$
  layer and its restatement as a generic crossover proposition were removed.
- The fixed-step theorem now states one inequality. The following paragraph
  interprets its right-hand side as retained population-gradient alignment
  minus quadratic smoothness cost; only then is the shorthand $B_k$ defined.
- The IS--PPO crossover is stated directly in both MSE and alignment forms.
  The MSE, certificate, and safe certificate oracles follow only after that
  implication is established.

## Terminology and scope

- The rendered manuscript and all included theory-validation figures use
  `IS`, not the manuscript-local label `Raw` or `Unmodified`.
- ESS is presented as control of the IS reliability radius, not as the full
  gradient MSE or as proof that PPO is preferable.
- The certificate comparison remains statewise and one-step. No cumulative
  optimality or independent ESS-threshold transfer is claimed.
- No experimental value or reported crossover was changed.
