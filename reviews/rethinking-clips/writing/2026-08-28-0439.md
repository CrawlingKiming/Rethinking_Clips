# Writing and claim-evidence review: full certificate

**Run:** 2026-08-28 04:39 UTC
**Verdict:** PASS IN SCOPE
**Open issues:** 2 limitations, both disclosed in the paper

## Scope

Reviewed the revised estimator-risk/full-certificate theory, the exact
Optdigits common-state experiment, front-matter claims, conclusion, figure
caption, and appendix protocol. Cross-checked every new empirical number against
`simulation/results/optdigits_full_certificate_summary.json` and
`simulation/results/optdigits_full_certificate_ess_proxy.json`.

## Five-dimension self-review

1. **Contribution — pass.** The paper now separates estimator accuracy from
   fixed-step update quality and supplies an exact counterexample in which PPO
   lowers MSE while Raw retains the stronger full certificate.
2. **Writing clarity — pass.** ESS, MSE, full certificate, pairwise oracle, safe
   oracle, and cumulative-return oracle are explicitly distinguished. The
   Optdigits paragraph states that `N=320` is the iid estimator minibatch size.
3. **Experimental strength — pass in scope.** All moments are exact over
   3,823-by-10 context-action pairs, the global smoothness condition is checked,
   and the principal Raw-to-PPO-to-no-op transition is numerically identified.
4. **Evaluation completeness — limitation disclosed.** The ESS proxy audit is
   adjacent-grid interpolation on one deterministic path. Full-path and
   focused-branch results are both reported, including balanced accuracy and
   PPO recall.
5. **Method soundness — pass for the statewise claim.** The full certificate is
   a one-step lower bound. The paper explicitly does not claim that its oracle
   maximizes cumulative return or transfers unchanged to an independent
   optimization trajectory.

## Claim-evidence map

- **Claim:** Lower PPO MSE need not imply a stronger policy update.
  **Evidence:** At rho 0.5577, PPO MSE is 0.0069647 versus Raw 0.0069848, but
  Raw certificate is 2.02e-5 versus PPO 5.99e-6.
  **Status:** supported.
- **Claim:** The full certificate has a positive Raw-to-PPO crossover.
  **Evidence:** At rho 0.5489, the MSE reduction 1.85e-4 exceeds the required
  1.82e-4; both certificates are positive and PPO is larger.
  **Status:** supported.
- **Claim:** A safe oracle should abstain after certification is lost.
  **Evidence:** At rho 0.5342 both candidate certificates are nonpositive and
  the null action has certificate zero.
  **Status:** supported.
- **Claim:** ESS can proxy the pairwise crossover on the controlled path.
  **Evidence:** The full-path forced Raw/PPO gate obtains 495/500 evaluation
  agreement and 0.994 balanced accuracy.
  **Status:** supported only for path-specific interpolation.
- **Claim:** A monotone ESS gate is a global safe oracle.
  **Evidence:** Full-path safe balanced accuracy is 0.778 with PPO recall 3/9
  because of a disconnected high-ESS PPO interval.
  **Status:** not supported and explicitly rejected in the text.

## Remaining limitations

1. Independent trajectories are still required to establish ESS-gate transfer.
2. A local certificate does not establish the best cumulative optimization
   trajectory; a separate path-dependent analysis or experiment is required.
