# Writing review: Gaussian theory-validation chain

**Status:** PASS IN SCOPE

## Reverse outline

1. Section 4.1 factors the IS second moment into normalized ESS and an
   importance-weighted contribution scale, yielding a finite-moment version of
   the reliability radius and safe-step theorem.
2. Section 5.1 checks the reliability event and improvement inequality before
   comparing alternative estimators.
3. The Gaussian specialization computes the Corollary 5 margin separately for
   TIS-3 and PPO against IS.
4. The quadratic objective turns those certificate comparisons into exact
   expected one-step policy-improvement comparisons.
5. A final bridge distinguishes complete-weight truncation in the one-step
   bandit from factorwise TIS in sequence models.

## Scope and precision

- The manuscript states that $M_{2,k}$ also captures weight--contribution
  coupling; it does not attribute the Gaussian trend to ESS alone.
- Corollary 5 is described through pairwise comparisons against IS rather than
  as a three-way crossover.
- The population-known $M_2$ and adaptive step are presented as controlled
  theory validation, not as an empirical gate.
- The one-decision TIS oracle is explicitly separated from factorwise TIS in
  sequence models.
