# Writing review: Introduction and estimator diagnostics

**Status:** PASS IN SCOPE

## Reverse outline

1. The Introduction starts from rollout reuse in LLM post-training and the
   learning--control tradeoff created by ratio-based safeguards.
2. The TIS-3/TIS-5 trajectories pose the delayed-failure question that
   population policy-deviation bounds do not answer.
3. The paper answers in two stages: full-trajectory IS reliability through
   ESS, then fixed-step selection among factorwise controlled estimators.
4. Section 5.1 gives an exact counterexample to MSE-only update selection and
   derives the IS--PPO--abstain ordering.
5. Section 5.2 tests temporal diagnostics under unclipped and TIS-3 updates;
   Section 6 then tests ESS-conditioned interventions.
