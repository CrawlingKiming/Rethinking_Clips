## Critical Issues

### C1: Prospective evidence is still described as observed evidence

- **Locations:** `main.tex:939--952`, `main.tex:1249--1279`
- **Exact text:** “Our empirical observation can be stated as a testable single-crossing property”; “the observed ranking reversal”; “validates the adaptive use of ESS experimentally”; “Their regime reversal is an empirical result”; “The value $0.1$ is an empirical crossover”.
- **Problem:** Section 7 contains a protocol and predictions rather than completed results. These sentences therefore conflict with the revised Abstract and Conclusion, which correctly describe $0.1$ as a candidate threshold requiring validation.
- **Fix:** Replace observed/result/validates language with hypothesis, candidate, or will test language until completed evidence is inserted.

### C2: Three implementation placeholders remain in the paper

- **Locations:** `main.tex:1040`, `main.tex:1157--1159`
- **Exact text:** `[EXACT CONSERVATIVE OBJECTIVE]`, `[EXACT OBJECTIVE]`, `[EXACT COEFFICIENT/RANGE]`, and `[EXACT CLIPPED OBJECTIVE]`.
- **Problem:** The algorithm and main comparison are not reproducible in their current form. These placeholders would be immediately visible to a reviewer.
- **Fix:** Define the aggressive and conservative losses and all coefficient ranges exactly before treating the draft as shareable.

## Major Issues

### M1: Systematic over-numbering of display equations

- **Evidence:** The document contains 80 numbered `equation`/`align` environments, 99 labels, and only 33 internal references. No referenced label is missing, but many labeled equations are never referenced.
- **Problem:** Numbering nearly every display increases visual noise and makes the genuinely important identities harder to find.
- **Fix:** Keep numbers for results and displays referenced later; convert purely local derivation steps to `equation*`, `align*`, or unnumbered display math.

### M2: Four uncited bibliography entries

- **Entries:** `agapiou2017intrinsic`, `chatterjee2018sample`, `thomas2015hcope`, `thomas2015hcpi`.
- **Problem:** These entries look like remnants of an earlier literature framing and make the bibliography appear insufficiently curated.
- **Fix:** Cite them where they materially support a claim or remove them.

## Minor Issues

### m1: Sample-ESS notation is not fully normalized

- **Locations:** `main.tex:119`, `1007--1074`, `1174`, `1422`.
- **Forms:** `\widehat\rho_{\mathrm{seq}}`, `\widehat\rho_\theta`, `\widehat\rho_{j,k}`, and unindexed `\widehat\rho`.
- **Fix:** State once that the indexed forms are context-specific instances of one raw sequence-ESS statistic, or use one base macro consistently.

### m2: Unused custom commands

- **Location:** `main.tex:32--52`.
- **Pattern:** Thirteen commands occur only in their definitions, including `\Pp`, `\KL`, `\TV`, `\rseq`, `\rhat`, `\what`, `\argmax`, and `\argmin`.
- **Fix:** Remove unused commands to reduce preamble noise, unless they are reserved for imminent sections.

### m3: Draft-instruction language remains

- **Locations:** `main.tex:705`, `main.tex:1149--1171`.
- **Exact text:** “This theorem formalizes the proposed story exactly”; “The empirical section must establish”; “Compare:”; “This is the key causal experiment.”
